# Dropship Tycoon

**Sims × Game Dev Tycoon, but you're a broke McDoodle's employee building a dropshipping empire.**

Find products on AliExprez, build your product page in a near-exact Shopifly admin, film or commission ad
creatives, media-buy on Fadbook and TikTak, read real-looking analytics, and decide when to scale, when to
kill, and when to refresh creatives. Pay rent, eat, sleep, work your shifts — then quit when the store can
carry you. Upgrade from your parents' basement to a penthouse.

▶ **Play:** https://neomorrison.github.io/dropship-tycoon/

- Real-world benchmarks drive the sim (CPM, link CTR, hook/hold rate, CVR, ATC, learning phase, attribution
  inflation, payouts, chargebacks) — see [`docs/BENCHMARKS.md`](docs/BENCHMARKS.md).
- Skill matters: page copy, pricing, creative hooks/angles and media-buying decisions are graded with real
  CRO & performance-marketing principles.
- Chill / Normal / Realistic difficulty. Saves live in your browser (export/import supported).

All brands are parodies. Not affiliated with Shopify, Meta, TikTok, AliExpress, or McDonald's.

## 3D world

Your place and McDoodle's are live 3D dioramas (three.js) when the browser has WebGL: you walk to the bed,
stove and desk, staff type at their desks, customers queue at the register. Rotate with the camera buttons or
<kbd>Q</kbd> / <kbd>E</kbd>, drag to spin, scroll to zoom, click the floor to walk, click yourself for your needs.
Settings has **3D room** (off = the painted rooms) and **Graphics** (Auto / High / Low). Without WebGL, or if
a model fails to load, the game quietly uses the painted rooms.

- Models are generated by headless Blender from `blender/` (see [`docs/3D.md`](docs/3D.md), the asset
  contract and runtime API in `src/three/`).
- The shell side lives in `src/ui/shell/scene3d/`: `SceneDirector` drives the stage from the game every frame,
  `director.ts` maps activities to what the player visibly does (unit tested), `mcdoodles.ts` runs the
  restaurant crowd, `LookEditor` is the create-a-sim step.
- QA: `node scripts/e2e/scene3d.mjs` (own dev server + GPU Chromium) screenshots every room, activity,
  McDoodle's at the lunch rush, night, the look editor and a phone into `scripts/e2e/out/scene3d/`.
